## The question we answer

**"Show me where work disappears, where decisions stall, and where preventable operational losses accumulate."**

Most teams already sense where things go wrong. A request arrives by phone and nobody owns it. A job waits for a decision that no one realises is theirs. The same information gets typed twice. Individually these look minor. Together they cost time, customers and money, and they are hard to see from inside the work.

This diagnostic makes those losses visible for one workflow, with evidence. It does not rely on opinion.

## What the diagnostic covers

The diagnostic is deliberately narrow, so it can finish quickly and the findings can be checked.

| | |
|---|---|
| **One workflow** | Chosen with you. For example: request received → assigned → completed → invoiced. |
| **One team or location** | The people who actually run that workflow. |
| **Duration** | About three weeks from the agreed start date. |
| **Your time** | One kickoff (60 min). Up to five short conversations with staff (about 45 min each). One read-out (60 min). |
| **Information used** | What you already have: exports from your ticketing, job or scheduling tool, sample paperwork, and staff descriptions of the steps. We do not need access to your systems. |

## What you receive

1. **Current-state workflow map.** Every step and handoff in the agreed workflow, confirmed with your process owner.
2. **Loss register.** Where work disappears, waits, repeats or needs avoidable manual intervention. Each entry says what the evidence is.
3. **Evidence and uncertainty register.** For every finding: what it rests on (your data, a staff account, an observation) and how confident we are. Unmeasured items are labelled as unmeasured.
4. **Prioritised opportunities.** The top three, each with a rough effort level and an expected-impact range, stated as estimates.
5. **One bounded improvement proposal.** A single concrete change, including how to test whether it works: what to measure, the baseline, and for how long.
6. **Implementation scope estimate.** What carrying out the proposal would involve, if you choose to.
7. **Read-out session.** We walk through the findings with you and agree what, if anything, needs revising.

A sample report outline is in *05 — Sample Deliverable Structure*.

## How success is agreed — before we start

Before any diagnostic work begins, we agree on writing what "done" means: the deliverables and a short list of acceptance criteria (see *04 — Outcome Specification Template*). Those criteria do not change after work begins. If the report falls short of one, we revise the report. We do not rewrite the criteria. You decide whether to accept the result.

## What this diagnostic does not include

- Building, configuring or integrating any software or automation.
- Access to your systems, passwords or credentials.
- Evaluating individual employees' performance. Findings describe the workflow, not people.
- Legal, financial, tax, HR or regulatory advice.
- Guaranteed savings. Impact figures are estimates until measured.
- Collecting your customers' personal information. We ask for exports with personal details removed.
- Ongoing support after the read-out, unless separately agreed.
