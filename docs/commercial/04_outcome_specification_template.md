## Acceptance criteria (fixed at agreement)
Each criterion is either checked directly or judged by the named person. Delete or adjust before agreement, never after.

| ID | Criterion | How it is checked | Who confirms |
|---|---|---|---|
| AC-1 | The workflow map includes every step and handoff named in the scoping conversations. | Process owner reviews the map against their own understanding. | Customer process owner |
| AC-2 | Every entry in the loss register states its evidence (data, staff account or observation) and a confidence level. | Check each row has both. | Either party |
| AC-3 | Anything unmeasured is labelled "unmeasured". No estimate is presented as a measurement. | Read the evidence and uncertainty register. | Either party |
| AC-4 | Each of the top three opportunities has an effort level and an impact range, marked as estimates. | Check the opportunities section. | Either party |
| AC-5 | The improvement proposal has a validation plan: what to measure, the baseline, and the duration. | Check the proposal section. | Either party |
| AC-6 | No finding evaluates an individual employee. | Read the report. | Customer process owner |
| AC-7 | The report contains none of the customer's clients' personal details. | Read the report and appendices. | Either party |
| AC-8 | Deliverables arrive by the agreed read-out date, or a new date is agreed in writing. | Dates. | Either party |
