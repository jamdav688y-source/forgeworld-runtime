## 1. What we ask permission for

With your agreement, we would:

1. **Talk with you and up to five staff members** about how the agreed workflow runs. Taking part is voluntary for each person.
2. **Review documents and exports you choose to share** about that workflow, such as request logs, job tickets or scheduling exports.
3. **Keep notes and a record of what we were told and shown,** so every finding can be traced to its source.

We will ask before recording any conversation. We will not record a conversation without explicit agreement from everyone present.

## 2. What we ask you **not** to send

- Your customers' names, contact details, addresses, payment details or account numbers. Please remove or replace these before exporting.
- Health, financial, legal or other sensitive personal information about anyone.
- Passwords, access keys or system credentials. We never need access to your systems.
- Anything you are not permitted to share.

If something like this arrives by mistake, we will tell you, delete it, and not use it.

## 3. How we use what you share

- **Only** to produce the diagnostic described in the agreed scope.
- **Not** to evaluate or report on individual employees. Findings describe the workflow.
- **Not** shared with anyone outside the project team without your written permission.
- **Not** used in case studies, marketing, training material or public content without separate, explicit, written permission. You may refuse with no effect on the diagnostic.
