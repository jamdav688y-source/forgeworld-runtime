# Artistic Review

Stated plainly, per this project's evidence laws: this review will not
claim more than what was actually made.

## What this is

A 90-second, 24fps, deterministic procedural animation: a particle/
node-graph system ("the organism") whose density, connectivity, palette,
and camera framing evolve continuously across nine named scenes, driven
by twelve tracked scalar metrics (`CONTINUITY/cognitive_state_timeline.json`).
The palette moves from near-black indigo through blue, teal, green, amber,
to warm light across the film's duration. Audio is synthesized
oscillator/noise layers (`AUDIO/`), not recorded or sampled, developing
alongside the same metrics.

## What this is not

- Not generated by a video-generation model.
- Not directed, shot, or edited by a human filmmaker or animator.
- Not scored or sound-designed by a human composer/sound designer.
- Not narrated (see `AUDIO/audio_provenance.json` -- `NARRATION_REJECTED`,
  because no speech-synthesis tool was available and the project's own
  law against using narration to paper over unclear visuals ruled out
  faking it).
- Not built from any licensed or purchased visual/audio asset -- every
  pixel and every sample is generated by code in this release
  (`genome/`, `audio/synth.py`).

## Honest assessment

**Strengths**: the continuity system genuinely works -- see
`transition_contact_sheet.jpg`, where the frame immediately before and
immediately after every one of the 8 scene boundaries is visually
near-identical, which is what "causal transition, not a jump cut" is
supposed to look like. The palette and node-density progression across
`contact_sheet_16x9.jpg` is a real, continuous visual arc, not nine
disconnected looks. Audio dynamics genuinely build and recede across the
90 seconds (`waveform_final_mix.png`) rather than sitting at one flat
level.

**Limitations**: the visual vocabulary is narrow -- one recurring motif
(a glowing node cluster on a gradient field) reskinned per scene via
color/density/a small per-scene accent shape. There is no character, no
represented environment, no cinematography in the sense of shot
composition choices beyond a single camera-distance/drift parameter. The
overall effect reads as abstract data-visualization motion graphics, not
as cinema in the sense of narrative filmmaking.

## Verdict

This is a real, working, technically-compliant, honestly-labeled
procedural artifact -- not a demonstration of directed artistic or
cinematographic craft. Anyone evaluating it as "a film" in the
traditional sense should calibrate expectations accordingly before
watching.
