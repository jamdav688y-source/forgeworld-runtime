This is a private space to think, not a program that judges you. It will walk you through a few short questions, one at a time.

  • Nothing you type leaves this device.
  • No account, no login, no tracking.
  • You can skip any question, or stop at any time.

This tool is not therapy, medical treatment, diagnosis, a sobriety score, or a crisis system. It only helps you organize your own thinking. If you are in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or a crisis line for your area.
