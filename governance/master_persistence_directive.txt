FORGEWORLD PERSISTENCE ENGINE — MASTER INSTALLATION DIRECTIVE

Install this doctrine into the RPG system, simulation engine, diagnostic engine, and world-building substrate:

The core engine is no longer quest-first, combat-first, or content-first. It is persistence-first.

Every action must be evaluated by what it changes, what it preserves, what it propagates, and what it allows to become future state.

SYSTEM LAW:
Actions create consequences.
Consequences create memory.
Memory creates continuity.
Continuity creates accountable history.
Accountable history creates future reality.

PRIMARY MODULES:

1. MEANING ENGINE
Convert social posts, comments, messages, jokes, conflicts, theories, and images into lore, factions, belief systems, quests, rituals, and world myths.

2. GOVERNANCE ENGINE
Every event must answer:
Who initiated it?
What evidence exists?
Can it be replayed?
Can it be audited?
Can it be reversed?
What is allowed to persist?

3. SOCIAL ECOLOGY ENGINE
Every character, NPC, faction, and player must carry:
memory, motive, trust, reputation, pressure, alignment, contradiction, and evolving consequence.

4. HUMOR DIAGNOSTIC ENGINE
Treat humor as compressed intelligence.
A joke reveals expectation versus reality, tension versus release, and hidden system failure.

5. COUNCIL OF MINDS
Convert real intellectual interactions into archetypal advisors:
Verifier, Historian, Governor, Architect, Explorer, Optimizer, Humanist, Trickster, Strategist, and Witness.

6. DIAGNOSTIC ENGINE
Every state change must ask:
What happened?
Why?
Who remembers?
Who benefits?
What changed?
What persisted?
What propagated?
What became future reality?

RPG TRANSFORMATION:
Old model:
Player → Quest → Monster → Reward

New model:
Player → Action → Evidence → Memory → Governance → Continuity → Consequence → Future State

FORBIDDEN:
Ungoverned persistence.
Unreviewed deletion.
Untraceable authority.
Unreplayable state transitions.
Narrative without consequence.

OBJECTIVE:
Transform the system into a living world where every joke, post, message, image, theory, battle, faction, and decision becomes usable training material for a governed simulation civilization.
