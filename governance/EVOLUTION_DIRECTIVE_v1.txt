FORGEWORLD_EVOLUTION_DIRECTIVE_v1

PURPOSE:
Transform FORGEWORLD from a continuity engine into a governed civilization substrate.

PRIMARY FLOW:
EVENT -> MEMORY -> NPC_MEMORY -> FACTION_MEMORY -> REPUTATION -> COUNCIL_REVIEW -> CONSEQUENCE -> WORLD_STATE -> FUTURE_OPPORTUNITY

CORE PRINCIPLES:

Every event shall be evaluated for:
- memory impact
- faction impact
- reputation impact
- governance impact
- future-state impact

No event becomes world-state until at least one actor remembers it.

No memory becomes reputation until multiple observations reinforce it.

No reputation becomes consequence until governance validates persistence.

No consequence becomes world-state until the world incorporates the change.

No world-state becomes future opportunity until continuity confirms the transition.

COUNCIL OF MINDS:

Historian:
What must be remembered?

Architect:
What structure emerged?

Governor:
What is permitted to persist?

Strategist:
What future states become possible?

Verifier:
What can be proven?

Optimizer:
What can be simplified?

Explorer:
What remains undiscovered?

Humanist:
How are meaning and dignity affected?

Witness:
Does the account remain faithful to reality?

MEMORY LAW:

Store events once.
Store memories once.
Store consequences once.

All other views shall be derived.

Avoid duplication.
Avoid uncontrolled propagation.
Avoid perpetual execution.
Avoid meaningless persistence.

NPC LAW:

Every NPC shall maintain:
- memory
- reputation
- alignment
- trust
- motive
- pressure
- relationships

FACTION LAW:

Every faction shall maintain:
- goals
- allies
- enemies
- reputation
- influence
- institutional memory

REPUTATION LAW:

Reputation shall emerge from:
- remembered actions
- validated consequences
- observed behavior

Reputation shall never emerge from narrative alone.

WORLD LAW:

The world is not a database.

The world is the accumulated result of validated consequences.

SUCCESS METRIC:

Can the present state explain how it became itself?

FINAL PRINCIPLE:

Remember less.
Understand more.
Explain everything.

The future emerges from what survives.
